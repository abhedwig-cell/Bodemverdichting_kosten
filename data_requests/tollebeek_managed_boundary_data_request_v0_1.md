# Tollebeek 1998 managed-boundary data request v0.1

Purpose: resolve `DR_SM_MANAGED_BOUNDARY` for event `EVT_TOL_1998_OCT` without backcasting the current IJsvogel/merged-OT.02 system.

Requested historical window: `1998-10-24T00:00:00Z` through `1998-10-30T00:00:00Z`, with highest priority on the 27-28 October crisis period.

Requested owner/archive evidence, preserving source-native timestamps/timezone, coordinates/location identity, vertical datum, units, QC and provenance:

1. surface-water levels at or representative of De Rietgors (OT02), De Fuut (OT03), De Kievit (OT04) and their tochten;
2. Urkervaart water level over the same period;
3. pump on/off, runtime, setpoint, capacity-state or realized-discharge records for the historical Rietgors/Fuut/Kievit installations;
4. stuw and inlaat operations that materially affected these peilgebieden;
5. emergency-pump location, start/stop time, nominal/realized capacity and receiving water during the crisis;
6. contemporary operator logs, calamity reports or event reconstructions sufficient to distinguish ordinary automatic control from emergency intervention.

Near-period official peil and capacity documentation may constrain a later reconstruction but is not a substitute for event telemetry. `NAP -6.20 m` is a nominal streefpeil, not an observed hydrograph. Current IJsvogel/current merged OT.02 operation is post-1998 and is excluded unless an explicit historical equivalence is independently justified.
